Privacy Policy

PrezPoint  ·  Last updated: October 2026

PrezPoint does not collect, transmit, or store any personal data. Ever.

PrezPoint is a macOS menu-bar utility for presentation pointer effects. It runs entirely on your device. There are no accounts, no servers, no telemetry, and no network calls of any kind.

Information We Do Not Collect

  • No account or sign-in required
  • No usage analytics or telemetry
  • No crash reports sent anywhere
  • No advertising identifiers
  • No network connections — the app never contacts the internet

All preferences (pointer mode, hotkey, colors) are stored locally in NSUserDefaults and never leave your device.

macOS Hardware Permissions

PrezPoint requests two macOS permissions. Both are used locally only and are never associated with your identity or shared with third parties.

Screen Recording

Used exclusively by the Loupe magnification mode, which captures a small region of the screen around the cursor to draw a magnified view. The permission is requested lazily — only when you first switch to Loupe mode — and is never requested at launch. Screen content is processed in memory and immediately discarded; nothing is saved to disk or sent anywhere.

Input Monitoring

Used to detect when you hold a modifier key (e.g. Control) as the overlay activation hotkey. The implementation uses a listen-only CGEventTap that observes only modifier-flag changes. No keystrokes, characters, or typed content are ever read, logged, or stored. This is not keylogging — it watches one bit (modifier held/released) and ignores everything else.

Children's Privacy

PrezPoint does not collect any data from any user, including children under 13. The app has no online features and cannot communicate with external services.

Changes to This Policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects data handling, this page will be updated and the "Last updated" date above will reflect the change.
